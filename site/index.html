02 The Work Speaks

Details make the difference.

Anyone can lay tile. Setting it so every line runs true, every corner miters clean and every edge is waterproofed to last — that's the standard we hold on every job, big or small.

  • i.Precision layout — patterns centered and planned before the first cut
  • ii.Proper waterproofing — built to last, not just to pass inspection
  • iii.Clean finish work — level lines, tight grout, no shortcuts
  • iv.45° mitered stone edges — the slab folded down the face at a true miter, veining running straight through the corner. It is slow, unforgiving work, and it is the detail we are known for.
